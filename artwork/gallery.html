Characters

The Solo — Street Samurai

East Asian, wild spiked hair, left cyberarm with pop-up pistol. Heavy weapons specialist wrestling with rage and fading humanity. "Get rid of the Rage" is all he wants.

The Fixer — Deal Maker

East Asian, sharp mohawk, ritual scars. High-fashion warrenrat who lost everything through betrayal. Honor-bound deal-maker trying to get off the street.

Digitz — The Netrunner

Pacific Islander, urban flash, cool as ice. Former nomad pack runner turned edge runner. Hungers for fame, settles for survival. Interface Rank 5.

Ryla Vox — Investigative Journalist

Independent media, News 54. Ran the doctored footage before she knew the truth. Terrified but determined to expose the droneware conspiracy. The team's moral compass.

Dr. Yuki Voss — Underground Ripperdoc

Former Trauma Team combat surgeon gone independent. One of three people in Night City who can extract military Arasaka neural-interface cyberware. Pristine clinic hidden behind Tanaka's Fish Market.

Cpl. Reyes — Arasaka Recovery Team

Professional. Cold. "Just doing his job." Leads a six-person tactical squad hunting the players across Night City. Smart tactician who adapts after first contact. Base 16, HP 50.

Adventure 1: Cyber Drones

The Awakening — "Why are we on the news?"

Three mercenaries wake in a safehouse to discover themselves on television — security footage shows them massacring Arasaka scientists. They don't remember any of it. The 8-hour clock starts now.

The Pachinko Parlor — Hacking Under Neon

The netrunner jacks into a hidden port beneath a pachinko machine, downloading security footage while guards patrol the sensory overload of the gambling floor. Double jackpot as cover.

Level 17 — Assault on Arasaka Tower

The final assault on Arasaka's droneware control facility. Cyber drones, corporate security, and a massive signal transmitter that must be destroyed — even if it means 50 innocent casualties.

Adventure 2: Dead Signal

Dead Signal — The Escape

Rusted fire escape, collapsing metal, an AV searchlight sweeping the building face. The city sprawls far below. One figure hangs by one arm as the grating gives way. 18 hours until the signal locks.

Kojo's Safehouse — The Shipping Container

A converted shipping container in the dockyards. Kojo Asante, chain-smoking and guilt-ridden, reveals the Ricochet Mk.IV counter-battery system. Military hardware for a military problem.

The Depot Job — Northside Medical Heist

Breaking into a Trauma Team medical warehouse at 02:00 for the neural bridge decoupler. Motion sensors, guard dogs, a 4-minute AV response window. Kira's rules: 15 minutes, no kills, no pharma.

The Siege at Tanaka's Fish Market

Reyes' team closes in while Voss operates. Snipers on rooftops, breach teams at exits, EMP pulse. Udo the full-borg holds the entrance. One player on the table, two to defend. 90 minutes per extraction.

The Extraction — Pulling the Droneware

Thread-thin cybernetic filaments being pulled from the base of a skull. Sinister black nodes with blinking lights. The patient is conscious, gritting teeth. Freedom from corporate control, one wire at a time.

Setting & Mood

Night City — The Urban Sprawl

Corporate arcologies pierce the smog layer. Holographic ads cover every surface. AVs cruise between towers. Somewhere down in the maze of neon and concrete, three people are running out of time.

DRONEWARE MK.III — Technical Schematic

Illegal military cyberware threaded along the spine and into the brain stem. Signal receiver, motor override nodes, memory suppressor, neural bridge. Full remote takeover with complete memory wipe.
