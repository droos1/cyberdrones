# PART A — READ-AHEAD & INSPIRATION

*Read once before the session. Not for the table.*

## Where we left off

The team drove two sealed Arasaka droneware units south to sell at **Costa Muerta**, the monthly black-market biotech auction on the rig-city of **Nueva Costa**, timed to Día de los Muertos. As played in Session 1: rolling in the **cargo van**, fought off **Don Bicho** (he *survived* — a live grudge on the road home), held the **Snake's Nest** with only **minor injuries**, banked strong **Aldecaldos** goodwill (but **declined the snake tattoo**), and crossed into **Tijuana**. They carry **Mateo's letter** to Tía Esmé.

*Confirm before play:* camp-defense tier (was Reyna's cousin among the wounded?), Fredrik's installed chrome, Kojo's brokerage %, current HP/Humanity.

## The one thing that makes this session make sense

**You don't negotiate with buyers, then also auction. You earn the *right to sell*, then the auction is real.**

- **Before the sale:** the house (the Costa Muerta syndicates) vets the sellers — are they legit, are they cops, will they fold? That vetting is Doña Esperanza's **worm-mezcal vision-quest** and the **exhibition bout.** Pass, and you're cleared to put the lot on the floor.
- **The auction is a genuine auction** — open bids. The players' agency is in *shaping the room*: set the reserve, hype the lot, spot the shill, and make **one call at the hammer** — the top cash number, or a rival's lower bid **plus a relationship**. That live choice is the whole "whose card do you carry" theme; it no longer happens on paper in advance.
- **The rig is cartel-secured, so the fight isn't there.** The players can't out-gun a syndicate fortress and shouldn't try. The danger is the one place they're isolated and decisive: **the helipad, on the way out.**

## The cast (lean — five people carry the session)

- **Ofelia "Ofi" Castañeda** — the handler. Early 40s, runs a flower shop that's really grief-logistics for a cartel city (she buries a lot of people's sons; launders through funeral wreaths). Dry, unsentimental, allergic to amateurs. *Not* muscle, *not* a face — she's logistics and consequences. Her whole arc is watching these northerners agree to things she can't get them out of.
- **Doña Esperanza Vargas** — Los Curanderos matriarch, ripperdoc-syndicate boss, religious in a dark way, rules from a deconsecrated cathedral. **She runs the vetting** (the worm-mezcal ritual is pure her) **and she's also a bidder.** The house that judges you also wants your lot. Evaluates people like cattle; means what she says.
- **Reyna Solano "La Viuda"** — cyber-lucha star. Sandevistan + monomolecular fan. Broke, wants out of the circuit, Aldecaldos-connected. Fredrik's opponent in the "friendly" bout → future ally. She fights *honorable*; the danger in the ring is the *setup*, not her.
- **Mr. Vega** — Militech cutout. Smooth, ex-SF, knows their NC file cold. One approach, one offer: the extradition shield. Selling to him makes them Militech assets — they call, you answer.
- **Tía Esmé Aldecaldo** — Aldecaldos rep, already warm (Mateo's letter + the camp defense). Low cash, priceless family bond. She just *greets* them at the rig — no cold pitch needed.
- **Iku Tanaka** *(the trap)* — Arasaka counterintel. Registered as a buyer but his story doesn't check out. **Glimpsed in the vision-quest** as a cold, wrong presence; **absent from the auction** = the tell. Coordinates the Section-9 raid from a submersible. Never meant to buy — only to find the lot and seize it.

## Vision-quest — inspiration bank

It's a **neural interrogation in a poncho** — a BD-worm sim Doña walks with them as psychopomp, not literal magic. Día-de-Muertos dreamscape: a marigold road, a *mercado de los muertos*, ancestors as data-ghosts. It strips social armor so she can read intent. Give players real choices in the haze. Vignettes to pick from:
- **The marigold road** — walk it without looking back; who looks back, and at what?
- **The market of the dead** — a vendor offers to buy one of their memories. What's it worth? What do they refuse to sell?
- **Your own altar** — each PC sees an altar with their photo on it. Who's standing at it mourning? (Fredrik's is the gut-punch — tie to his therapy arc.)
- **The cold guest** — one soul in the market casts no shadow and mourns no one. That's Tanaka's echo. A curious player can clock it.

## Pacing

Full session. Natural intermission after the bout (Tijuana done, before the chopper). **If running long:** compress the chip-up to two lines, and drop the auction's hype sub-game — go straight to bids and the hammer. Don't cut the vision-quest or the extraction; they're the tentpoles.

# PART B — RUN SHEETS

## 1 · Wake at Ofelia's
*Set the board · RP · ~10 min*

**Setup:** Morning above the flower shop, Zona Norte. Marigolds everywhere for the festival. The units are in Ofelia's floor-safe.

> *"Kojo says treat you like family. So — family rules. The apartment's yours till the sale. The shop's mine. Steal a carnation and I shoot you in the femoral. Now sit, because you don't understand how this works yet."*

**What's going on:**
- Ofi lays out the reframe (players learn it here): **you can't just sell.** The Costa Muerta syndicates vet every seller of a hot lot. No blessing, no floor.
- Two gates before they're cleared: **(1)** sit with **Doña Esperanza** today — "she'll read you"; **(2)** whatever she asks of you after.
- The sale itself is a **real auction on the rig, tomorrow night.** House takes **12%** of cash. Non-cash favors aren't taxed.

**Player beats:**
- Questions about the buyers → give them **The Board** (Part C) as intel Ofi rattles off.
- Anyone pushing to skip the vetting and deal privately: Ofi shuts it down flat — *"Walk a sale around the house and they feed you to the rig."*

**Exits:** Ofi drives them to the cathedral for the sit-down with Doña. → **Sheet 2.**

## 2 · The Vetting
*Credibility gate + seed the trap · RP / neural set-piece · ~30 min*

**Setup:** Doña's deconsecrated cathedral. Candles, a ripperdoc bay behind the altar, a bottle of black mezcal with a live cyber-worm coiled at the bottom. She pours four.

> *"Words lie. Blood lies. The worm does not. Drink with me, and let me see who came into my house."*

**What's going on:**
- The **worm is a BD-jack.** Swallow it and the room dissolves into a shared Día-de-Muertos dreamscape (see inspiration bank). Doña walks it with them. **It's her lie-detector.** Refuse = fail the vetting (no sale).
- She's checking three things: *are they real sellers, are they a plant, will they break.*

**Player beats (give agency in the haze):**
- **Confession** — an ancestor-figure (or Doña) asks each PC what they *truly* want from this sale. **Honesty is safe and builds her trust.** Lying to her sim: DECEPTION vs her Insight (**DV 17**) — fail and she reads it, and turns cold (worse auction posture, no floor-waiver later).
- **The cold guest** — a soul in the market casts no shadow, mourns no one. Clock it (HUMAN PERCEPTION or INTERFACE **DV 15**) → they've spotted **Tanaka's echo.** He's registered to bid but he is *wrong.* (This is the early trap seed — reward it.)
- **The bargain** — euphoric, loose, Fredrik is offered a "friendly game — show us your prowess." He agrees. A lucid PC can grab the reins (WILL/COOL **DV 15**) to set terms or dodge — **but dodging reads as fear and costs credibility** (Doña's read drops a tier). Best play: agree, look strong.

**Exits:** They surface hours later, cleared to sell. Fredrik is booked on tomorrow's Día lucha card. Doña's read of them (warm / guarded / cold) is a dial you carry into the auction. → **Sheet 3.**

## 3 · "Oh My God"
*Stakes reveal + prep · RP · ~10 min*

**Setup:** Back at the shop. Ofi makes a call, goes white, hangs up.

> *"A friendly exhibition. Ay, Dios. You agreed to fight **La Viuda** — top of the card. And the promoters are taking bets on the northerner getting cut. 'Friendly.' They 'forgot' to lock her blade."*

**What's going on:**
- The bout is billed non-lethal, but Doña quietly let Reyna's **monomolecular fan stay live** — she wants to see Fredrik under *real* threat. The betting circuit smells blood on the outsider.
- Ofi scrambles to even the odds. This is fast — Fredrik's already the fighter, so it's a top-up, not a shopping trip.

**Player beats — Ofi can source, quickly (cash or favor):**
- Arena-legal chrome for Fredrik he doesn't already run: **Big Knux** (100eb), a one-shot **Sandevistan** trigger (1,000eb), **Subdermal Armor** (1,000eb).
- **Intel on Reyna** (STREETWISE **DV 13**): Sandevistan opener, fan is her only lethal option, she fights *clean by choice* — she won't kill him if he gives her a way not to.
- Mats/Digitz plan their corner game (spot the blade, jam it, work the crowd — see Sheet 4).

**Exits:** That night → La Plaza Hueso. → **Sheet 4.**

## 4 · The Exhibition
*Fredrik's spotlight · light combat · ~30 min*

**Setup:** La Plaza Hueso, a converted bullring. 4,000 in face paint, brass band, sold out. **Doña watches from a private box** — she is scoring the *crowd*, not the falls. Two falls out of three.

**What's going on:**
- **Fredrik vs Reyna, singles.** Firearms/monoblades banned by rule — but her fan is live (Sheet 3). First blood reveals it.
- Reyna is a **glass cannon** who **will not kill him** — she pulls lethal strikes and telegraphs an honorable out. If Fredrik fights with honor (no dirty corner-work), she salutes him. If Mats/Digitz cheat blatantly, the *crowd* turns — and that's what Doña scores.
- **Reyna clocks them as Aldecaldos** on entrance (her cousin, from the camp) — *"¡Aldecaldos!"* — a fist raised across the ring. Faction goodwill, made public.

**Player beats:**
- **Fredrik (in ring):** run 3 narrative rounds, ~2 rolls each (his best combat skill vs Reyna's Evasion/attacks). Fall milestones, not HP-to-zero — he can't die here, he can get *cut.*
- **Corner (Mats/Digitz):** spot the live fan (PERCEPTION **DV 13**) → jam it (TECH/NET **DV 15**, removes the lethal threat) · rile the front row to break her focus (PERSUASION **DV 13**, +1 to Fredrik) · **any blatant assist loses the crowd.**

**Stat block:**

| Fighter | HP | SP | Attack | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reyna "La Viuda" | 35 | 9 | Cyberarm 3d6 · Sandevistan 1×/fight · fan (live) 4d6 | Fast, honorable, won't finish him |

**Outcome (what Doña saw):**
- **Won/lost with honor, crowd loud** → Doña impressed; the **lot gains standing** (opening bids +~10%). Reyna seeks them out after — wants a word, later.
- **Won dirty, crowd flat** → Doña: *"estilo, pero no honor."* Standing flat. Reyna cool.
- **Vega approaches** in the tunnel after, either way. → hands a card, one line: *"Interested parties from the north. We'll speak on the floor."* (His whole pre-auction footprint — that's it.)

**Exits:** Intermission point. Next day, chopper to the rig. → **Sheet 5.**

## 5 · Transit
*Scene-break · ~5 min, no rolls*

**Setup:** Seawall helipad at sundown. Ofelia is door agent; she keeps the sealed case and the **Ricochet loads onto the chopper** ("stays with the bird — house won't let it on the floor"). Buyers' reps board the same flight.

> *"Two units, sealed by me. The bird stays hot on the pad out there — my pilot doesn't cut engines till everyone's back or everyone's dead. Remember which one you'd rather be."*

**What's going on (planting the getaway):**
- 35 min west over the Pacific; Nueva Costa rises as a constellation of lashed-together oil platforms.
- **House rules, stated on arrival:** long guns checked at the armory (one concealed sidearm each; Fredrik's internal Pop-up VHP isn't "a gun"). The floor is patrolled by the **Pacers** (syndicate peacekeepers) — the players are *not* the security here and can't out-fight the house.
- Establish the geography they'll flee through later: **Salón → single caged gangway → helipad deck, chopper spooling.**

**Exits:** The Salón, Lot 4 coming up. → **Sheet 6.**

## 6 · The Auction
*The sale, with real agency · RP set-piece · ~40 min*

**Setup:** The *Salón de la Casa Muerta* — a converted hangar, skulls and marigolds, a masked auctioneer mic'd to the room. Droneware is **Lot 4**, sold as a sealed pair (Ofi's rule). **Tanaka's chair is empty.**

**Before bidding — players set terms (their levers):**
- **Reserve** — a cash floor below which they walk (failed lot returns, minus 5,000eb fee).
- **Pair or split** — Ofi advises pair; splitting invites the wrong buyers.

**During — each PC gets ONE room action:**
- **Mats — hype the provenance** (PERSUASION **DV 15**): sealed Arasaka R&D, Voss's own seal → pushes the whole field up a tier.
- **Fredrik — the arena rep** (intimidation/presence, **DV 13**): stares down a lowballing shill; discourages timid money.
- **Digitz — scan the room** (INTERFACE **DV 15**): flags a **shill bidder** driving price with no funds — *and* flags that **Tanaka's absence means a buyer with the location and no intent to pay.** (If they clocked his echo on Sheet 2, this lands as confirmation: *a raid is coming.*)

**The tell:** a sharp table should now be nervous — Tanaka knows where they are and isn't bidding. Let them decide to **expedite / prep the getaway.** That unease *is* the bridge to Sheet 7.

**The hammer — the choice (see The Board, Part C):**
- Top cash number, **or** a rival's lower bid **+ their relationship** (Militech shield / Aldecaldos bond / Curanderos clinic).
- **80% floor:** the accepted bid must be ≥80% of the top cash bid. **Tía Esmé's number is below it** — to take the Aldecaldos bond, either push her cash up (PERSUASION **DV 17**; the room just proved corps want this) or get a **floor-waiver from Doña** (**DV 15** if her read of them is warm — the vetting pays off here).

**Exits:** Hammer falls. Delivery completes **on landing back in TJ** — so the sealed case and the winning buyer's rep fly out *with the players.* They head for the helipad. → **Sheet 7.**

## 7 · The Extraction
*The tactical set-piece · hard combat · ~50 min*

**Setup:** The caged gangway to the helipad deck. Wind, sea-spray, night. Chopper spooling — **needs the case aboard + 3 rounds to lift.** As they step onto the deck, the rig's floods die: **Section-9 is boarding.** Tanaka's real play — seize the case before it leaves the rig.

```
   [ SALÓN exit ]
        |
   caged gangway   <-- chokepoint (1-wide)
        |
 ~~~~~~ HELIPAD DECK ~~~~~~
 [fuel drum][drum]     ((crane arm))
        .   CHOPPER (spooling, Ofi + pilot)  .
 [container]                    [container]
 --- railing --- ocean, 30m drop --- railing ---
   ▲ Section-9 fast-rope in + climb the rig legs
```
*(Printable tactical map: `map-helipad.svg`.)*

**Objectives:** get **the case + the winning buyer's rep + all PCs** aboard and **lift off** before Section-9 secures the deck. Losing the case = the sale unwinds (payment clawed back, ally's trust gone).

**What matters tactically (reward smart play):**
- **The Ricochet is on the chopper** — reach it and Digitz can slave it (INTERFACE **DV 13**) → deck-sweeper, turns the fight.
- **Kill the Team Lead's micro-drone** (called shot / NET) → Section-9 loses its coordination + spotting (drop their effective attacks).
- **Fuel drums** = 6d6 AoE if shot. **Crane arm** = Digitz can swing it (**DV 15**) to sweep the gangway. **Gangway** = a 1-wide chokepoint the players can hold.
- **The rig disarmed them** — sidearms + Fredrik's VHP + melee only until they reach the Ricochet. Real constraint; makes the chopper the prize.
- **Their ally fights** (depends on who they sold to):
  - **Vega** → his staffer reveals a Sandevistan + 4d6 smart-pistol — *terrifying.*
  - **Tía Esmé** → two Aldecaldos cousins, steady guns.
  - **Doña** → her bodyguard; Doña herself will take a hit to save "her property."
  - **Clean/anonymous bidder** → **no ally.** You chose cash over friends — you fight alone. (Let this land.)

**Clock:** Round 1 lights out + first contact · Rounds 2–3 breach the deck, drone spots, grenades on the gangway · Round 3+ chopper is flight-ready — every round on the deck after is a grenade/Sandevistan rush for the case · **Section-9 breaks if they lose 4, or immediately if the Lead falls.**

**Stat block:**

| Type | SP | HP | Weapon | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section-9 Operator ×5 | 15 | 45 | SMG 4d6 · frag · Sandevistan | Coordinated; never freelances |
| Section-9 Team Lead | 17 | 55 | Tech-rifle 5d6 · micro-drone (spotter) | Kill drone → team loses coordination |

*If the players tipped Tanaka off (caught him and showed it), add **2 operators.*** Tanaka stays on the sub — unreachable — and pulls out the moment the grab fails.

**Exits:** Lift-off. Section-9 tags the chopper with a tracker dart (AWARENESS **DV 15** to find & dump it, or accept Tanaka knows where they land). → **Sheet 8.**

## 8 · Aftermath
*Payoff + Adv 4 threads · RP · ~10 min*

**The chopper lifts.** Below, the Pacers are dragging Section-9 bodies off the gangways; the Lead is dead, the survivors are the syndicates' prisoners now. Tanaka's sub is already gone.

**Payout (net after 12% house cut; see Part C for the board):**
- Sold to **Vega** → ~**176,000eb + Militech extradition shield** (you're an asset now — they call, you answer)
- Sold to **Doña** → ~**132,000eb + Curanderos clinic-for-life**
- Sold to **Tía Esmé** (negotiated up) → ~**132,000eb + Aldecaldos family bond**
- Sold to **clean bidder** → ~**185,000eb, no relationship** (and you fought the deck alone)

**Faction fallout:**
- **Whoever won** → strong ally if a relationship deal; cold-neutral if pure cash.
- **Tía Esmé / Aldecaldos** → warm regardless (the letter, the camp, Reyna's salute). Bond unlocked if sold to her.
- **Tanaka / Arasaka** → hostile but *bloodied.* Section-9 took a real loss; they back off a while. They have the team on file now.
- **Don Bicho** → still out there, twice-humiliated, owns the road north. Unresolved.
- **Reyna** → free agent, honors the team, sends a painted cyber-mask: *"For when you next come south."*
- **Kojo** → paid, delighted, sends a *handwritten* thank-you (he's never handwritten anything).
- **Lazard** → Kojo's word two days on: audit suspended, "reassigned" to an off-grid Arasaka site. Deniably retired. Arc closes; the name is a case file now.

**Closing beat — the Day of the Dead.** Back in TJ by evening, the parade fills the streets. Fredrik stops at a stranger's altar and looks a long time at photographs of the dead — he hasn't built one for anyone in years. He should think about that. Mats buys flowers. Digitz calls his mentor.
