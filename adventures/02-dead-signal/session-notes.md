## What Happened

**Part 1 — Fire Escape:** Clean enough escape from the Arasaka building.

**Part 2 — Kojo:** Mats came into the meeting expecting to be set up. He pushed Kojo hard on the trust question — and rolled perfectly on the negotiation. Kojo went from "I'll give you 24 hours and that's it" to fully backing the team. That perfect roll unlocked the **Ricochet Mk.IV counter-battery system** plus other operational support throughout the rest of the adventure. This will pay dividends going forward — Kojo is now a real ally, not a sketchy broker.

**Part 3 — Mole's warning:** Delivered as written. The Mole subsequently went dark and has now fully exfiltrated from Arasaka (status: out, location unknown).

**Part 4 — Voss:** Met as written. Agreement struck.

**Part 5 — Depot job:** Pure stealth approach. The cyberdogs were **dosed with tranquilizers** rather than killed or evaded — clean, no alarms, Kira's contact preserved, Trauma Team is **not** a hostile faction. Decoupler acquired without incident.

**Part 7 — Siege:** Reyes' team came in as scripted. The clinic defense held. The droneware extractions all completed. Reyes' kill team was **repulsed** (Lazard's mobile transmitter situation TBC — assume neutralized given the team was driven off).

**Part 8 — Aftermath:**

- Gave **1 droneware unit to Dr. Voss as payment** for the surgery (Voss banked her own piece of leverage / research material)
- Plan for the remaining 2: **sell on the black market for cash** — this is the seed of Adventure 3
