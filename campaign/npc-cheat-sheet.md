# NPC Cheat Sheet

Quick-reference card for major NPCs across the campaign. For full stat blocks and motivations, see the source adventure (`adventures/NN-<slug>/adventure.md`). PC info: `player-characters.md`.

## Party Allies & Warm Contacts

### Night City

- **Kojo Asante** *(chain smoker, gold rings, runs from Suki's basement)* — Mats's fixer. **Strong ally** post-Adv 2 (earned via Mats's perfect negotiation roll). Brokered the southern route, takes 10% of the Costa Muerta proceeds. *Adv 2+*
- **Dr. Yuki Voss** *(precise, 50s, ex-Trauma Team surgeon; clinic behind Tanaka's Fish Market)* — Ripperdoc. **Even** — paid in full with one droneware unit in Adv 2; respects them. Did all the team's chrome work. *Adv 2+*
- **Udo** *(massive full-borg, reads paperbacks)* — Voss's bodyguard. Friendly by association. *Adv 2+*
- **Ryla Vox** *(trench coat, recording cybereye)* — Independent journalist, News 54. **Ongoing contact.** Sitting on the level-17 long-form, names kept out. *Adv 1+*
- **The Mole** *(older, glasses, R&D bone structure)* — Former Arasaka R&D scientist. **Exfiltrated** via Trauma Team — silently confirmed at the border crossing in Adv 3. No further contact expected. *Adv 1, 2, brief Adv 3 cameo*

### South of the Border

- **Ofelia "Ofi" Castañeda** *(late 30s, perpetual cigarette, flower-shop front in Zona Norte)* — Kojo's Tijuana fixer. **Friendly via Kojo.** Runs the team's TJ safehouse. Sharp, fast, doesn't sleep. *Adv 3*
- **Mateo Cruz** *(60s, snake tattoos, burned face, chess set in his office)* — Aldecaldos waystation elder at the Snake's Nest. **Goodwill** if the camp was defended. Gives the team the border coin + an introduction to Tía Esmé. *Adv 3*
- **Tía Esmé Aldecaldo** *(50s, gray-streaked braid, snake-mark coin)* — Senior Aldecaldos family member, Mateo's cousin. **Aldecaldos family bond** if she got a droneware unit; warm regardless. *Adv 3*
- **Reyna Solano "La Viuda"** *(masked lucha star, Sandevistan + monomolecular fan blade)* — Top of the Tuesday cyber-lucha card. Wants out of the syndicate. **Potential future ally** — sent the party a painted cyber-mask post-Adv 3. *Adv 3*
- **Cati** *(~12, Aldecaldos kid, runs ammo)* — Soft NPC tie at the Snake's Nest. Gave Fredrik a steel pendant during the camp defense. *Adv 3*

## Transactional / Neutral

- **Kira Nantes** *(nervous, fast, professional)* — Trauma Team logistics dispatcher. Owed Voss for her daughter's life. Stealth depot job preserved her. **Friendly via Voss; do not burn.** *Adv 2*
- **Comandante Castillo** *(small, weathered, military formal; ex-Federal officer)* — Runs the old US-MX border crossing like a fiefdom. **Respects the Aldecaldos coin; accepts cash bribes.** *Adv 3*
- **Doña Esperanza Vargas** *(older, missionary-cool, ceramic-cup coffee, runs Curanderos from a deconsecrated church)* — Los Curanderos cartel matriarch. **Strong ally → permanent clinic privileges** if she got a droneware unit; respectful neutral if not. *Adv 3*
- **Mr. Carlos Vega** *(linen suit, no visible chrome, ex-special-forces poise)* — Militech cutout (almost certainly). **Asset relationship** if he got a unit (will call in the future); cold neutrality if outbid. *Adv 3*

## Hostile / Active Threats

- **Lazard** *(Arasaka exec, droneware program lead)* — **Alive but deniably retired** to an off-grid Pacific Northwest Arasaka facility after Adv 3. Functionally finished as a personal threat; name lives on as a sealed case file. *Adv 1–3*
- **Cpl. Reyes** *(cybered tactical operator, runs/ran a tight 6-man squad)* — Arasaka recovery team lead. **Kill team repulsed at the clinic** (Adv 2); his personal fate TBC. *Adv 2*
- **Iku Tanaka** *(40s, Japanese, Tokyo-Arasaka dialect, Arasaka watch logo)* — Arasaka counterintelligence under deep cover as a Pacific Rim "medical buyer." **Hostile** — escaped on submersible OR captured at the Costa Muerta auction. *Adv 3*
- **Don Bicho "El Cien Patas"** *(cybered sadist, mantis-blade cyberarm, Sandevistan one-shot)* — Raider warlord, I-5 / Free Fire Zone corridor. **Recurring road threat** for any return north. *Adv 3*
- **Arasaka Section-9 / Special Recovery Division** — Black-ops counterintel branch. **Took operational-scale losses at Costa Muerta**; will back off short-term but holds a long file on the team. *Adv 3*
